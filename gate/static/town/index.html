Attest in NANDA Town

Attest is a bouncer for AI agents. Before one robot does business with another, Attest checks the ID.

The buyer robotAn AI agent that wants a price, or wants to place an order.
The shop robotAn AI agent that sells things. It will not deal with a robot it can't identify.
Attest, the bouncerChecks three things: who is this robot, which company answers for it (and put money down), and is it allowed to do this.

NANDA Town is the class's test town where AI agents meet and trade. Everything below is a real recorded run there, not a mock-up.
