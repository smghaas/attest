Attest

ID checks for AI agents
connecting… · gate key …

Before your AI agent does business with another AI agent, Attest checks its ID.

  • Who is this robot? It must carry an ID signed by a real company.
  • Who answers for it? That company has put money down. If its robot cheats, it loses some.
  • Is it allowed to do this? The company said what it may do, and up to how much.

Try it yourself

Four steps, about a minute. You play both sides: a company with a robot, and a shop that checks IDs. Nothing to install; the money here is pretend.

1. Make your company

2. Give it a robot (an AI agent)

3. Make a shop that checks IDs

4. Your robot asks the shop

5. Your robot's track record

Every deal your robot finishes is added to its permanent record, signed by both sides. Other shops can read it before trusting your robot. Nobody can edit it, not even us.
