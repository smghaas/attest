Use Attest in your browser

No install, no demo agents. Keys are made and kept in this browser; the gate only sees signatures. Go in order, or skip to step 4 with an existing receiver.

1. Your company

2. Your agent

3. Who checks (receiver)

4. Send a request, get a decision

5. Your agents' track record (on the ledger)

After the gate allows a request, press "Deal done" or "Dispute" under the decision. The agent and the receiver both sign, and it is added to the agent's history. Anyone can read it; nobody can edit it.
