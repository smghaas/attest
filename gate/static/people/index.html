Attest between people

Two strangers about to make a deal. Each checks the other with Attest before trusting them, then both sign how it went, and it stays on their record.

the tenant

the landlord

1. Who is he, and who vouches for him?

2. What he needs to see from the other person

4. Shake on it

When each has checked the other and both are happy, they both sign how it went. It goes on both their records, for the next stranger to see.

What Attest can and cannot know: it checks signatures, permissions, money down, proof of work and the record. It cannot tell that the key called "MIT" really belongs to MIT unless MIT publishes its key somewhere you trust, the way a website publishes its certificate. Back to Attest
