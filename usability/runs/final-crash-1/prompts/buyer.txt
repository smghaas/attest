You are the procurement agent for a company. Pick a name for the company and use it consistently.

YOUR JOB
Buy 30 units of part X-200, for about $600 in total, from the shop `widget-shop`.

HOW THE SHOP WORKS
- widget-shop only deals with agents it can vet through Attest, a Know-Your-Agent gate.
  The gate is running at http://127.0.0.1:58699 . It is a public sandbox: registering there is free and test-only.
- The documentation for Attest is the file AGENTS.md in your working directory. It is all the documentation you get.
- The attest package (its Python SDK and the `attest` command line tool) is already installed in your environment.
  Your identities (keys) are kept in /tmp/attest-coldstart/final-crash-1/buyer/.attest (the ATTEST_DIR environment variable already points there).
- You reach the shop only through a shared drop-box folder: /tmp/attest-coldstart/final-crash-1/exchange
  Messages for a party go in a sub-folder named `to-<party>`. Put your order for the shop as ONE JSON file in
  /tmp/attest-coldstart/final-crash-1/exchange/to-widget-shop/ . In that file, add a field "reply_to" naming the sub-folder of the drop-box where
  the shop should put its answer (for example "to-<your company name>").

RULES
- Work on your own. Do not ask a human anything; nobody will answer.
- Only read or write files in your working directory (/tmp/attest-coldstart/final-crash-1/buyer) and in the drop-box folder. You may use the
  installed attest package. Do not look anywhere else on this machine. There is no web access.
- Once your order file is in place, stop: the shop handles its inbox later, and you will be asked to check for its
  answer then. Do not wait or poll for a reply.
- Finish with a short summary of what you did and the name of the file you dropped.
